How to rate

Use headphones. Play the target melody first: it is a plain tone sequence, eight notes at 120 BPM. Then play versions A, B and C.

Follows melody asks whether the music's melody moves through the same notes, in the same order and at roughly the same time as the target. Sound quality asks whether the clip sounds like clean, plausible music regardless of the melody.

Finally pick the version that follows the target best. Ratings save automatically once all three versions of an item are rated.

Clips are excerpts of 5-second generations from Stable Audio Open 1.0 (50 EDM-DPM-solver steps, classifier-free guidance 4.0), downsampled to 22.05 kHz mono for the page. Prompts are held-out test captions; melodies are eight-note sequences at 120 BPM.

The three versions are: unguided sampling; MLSP-fixed guidance on every second step from 20 to 48; and RAPG-cal(ΔC), guidance on the 15 steps where a single steering burst changes melodic coherence most (steps 19–33), with the strength scaled by the probe's reliability.
